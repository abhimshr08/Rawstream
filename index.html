How to configure your cloud files for streaming:

01
Google Drive

Right-click the video -> Select Share -> Change General Access to "Anyone with the link can view". Copy the link.

02
Microsoft OneDrive

Select the video -> Click Share -> Ensure it's set to "Anyone with the link can view" -> Click Copy Link.

Note on Google Drive Quotas: If a very large video has been played many times recently, Google may temporarily limit streaming/downloads for that file.
